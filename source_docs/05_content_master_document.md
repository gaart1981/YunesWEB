## 3. Core company description

### 3.1 Short version

> Salimi Engineering is a Morocco-based, founder-led engineering and project advisory company supporting international and local clients with engineering coordination, owner’s representation and local project delivery.

### 3.2 Medium version

> Salimi Engineering supports international and Moroccan clients across project definition, engineering coordination, tendering, construction follow-up and local delivery. Each assignment is led by founder Yunes Salimi and supported by specialists selected for the technical requirements of the project.

### 3.3 Long version

> Salimi Engineering is a founder-led engineering and project advisory company based in Morocco. The company supports investors, engineering firms, contractors, developers and industrial clients with owner’s engineering, electrical and MEP engineering, technical coordination and local project delivery. Yunes Salimi brings international project experience developed through a long engineering career in Morocco and Russia. Each assignment is organised around the actual project scope, with direct founder involvement and project-specific specialist resources.

# 18. Translation rules

### English to French

- Prefer accepted Moroccan and international engineering terminology.
- Preserve `AMO` where commercially useful.
- Translate for business meaning, not word-for-word.
- Review terms such as:
  - owner’s engineering;
  - project delivery;
  - technical representation;
  - commissioning;
  - handover;
  - design review.

### English to Arabic

- Use professional Modern Standard Arabic suitable for B2B engineering.
- Preserve necessary technical abbreviations in Latin characters.
- Review directionality for emails, phone numbers and units.
- Do not use automatic translation without professional validation.
