# Implementation Decision Log

## 2026-08-12 — Business positioning and public email

**Status:** Owner-confirmed

### Decision

Salimi Engineering is positioned as a **small, founder-led engineering bureau in Morocco**, not as a solo freelance engineer and not as a large permanent consultancy.

The bureau may take on:

- focused engineering assignments;
- recurring project-support missions;
- medium-sized project scopes.

The delivery model is scalable. Salimi Engineering may engage or hire engineers and project specialists for a specific assignment according to the required disciplines, workload and site presence. The website may communicate this ability to build and expand a project team.

The founder and lead engineer remains directly accountable for technical quality and the client relationship while the delivery team scales around the project.

The exact permanent employee count is **not confirmed** and must not be stated or implied.

### Confirmed public contact fact

Business email:

`info@salimiengineering.com`

### Implementation consequences

- Remove or rewrite public copy that positions the business as an independent single engineer or one-person practice.
- Prefer company/bureau-first calls to action while keeping the founder visible as the accountable technical lead.
- Explain that the project team is dimensioned to the assignment and can be expanded with engaged or hired specialists.
- Do not invent named employees, permanent departments or a permanent headcount.
- Use the confirmed business email in public contact, footer, legal and privacy content.
- Keep prior professional experience clearly separated from Salimi Engineering company assignments.
